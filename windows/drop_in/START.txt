Saros for Windows x64

Extract the entire ZIP. Drag your Saros game folder onto Play Saros.exe.
You can also open Play Saros.exe and choose the folder, or place the folder
inside Game Files and double-click Play Saros.exe.

Use your extracted, decrypted PPSA07631 version 01.000.000 game files.
Choose the folder containing eboot.bin and sce_sys. Keep it in place.
The launcher prepares the game inside this kit, in Saros Windows. The first start
can take several minutes while shaders compile. For best disk use, extract the kit on
the same local NTFS drive as your game files.

On the first two screens, hold Enter, Space, or controller Cross for about
three seconds. In the Movement & Camera tutorial, use WASD to walk and the
mouse or right stick to look around. Open Play Saros.exe again to resume.
Your saves are in the prepared installation under play\_sd.
If the game closes unexpectedly, see last-game.log in that installation.

For other launchers, use --game PATH, --build-dir PATH, --fullscreen or
--windowed, --prepare-only, or --check. Run Play Saros.exe --help for details.
